About

I love writing on various topics, and I take real pleasure in gathering information and learning about new and exciting fields: society, consumption, mental health, gastronomy, marketing, among others.

That curiosity is really the thread running through my whole career. I started out in front-of-house administration: answering calls, keeping directors' diaries, organising filing systems. Over the years that grew into running the day-to-day of a busy legal practice, coordinating court schedules for 15 advocates, managing invoices and filing, supervising junior staff, and making sure nothing was missed.

Alongside that, I built two other things I'm proud of. The first is a tender preparation practice, Mojospan Enterprises, where I handle the full process for clients: filling in forms, compiling and printing supporting documents, binding the final submission, and making sure everything is reviewed, approved, signed and in on time. The second is legal research and content writing: I research cases for advocates, and I write well-researched, search-engine-friendly content for clients who need it done properly.

If your work needs someone organised, discreet and genuinely interested in getting the details right, that's what I do.
