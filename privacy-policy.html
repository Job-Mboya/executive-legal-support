Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Welcome to Monica Executive & Legal Support. Your privacy is important, and this Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored and protected when you visit this website or use the contact form.

This website provides information about executive and administrative support, legal research, content writing, tender preparation, procurement support and related professional services.

By using this website or submitting information through the contact form, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the contact form, the following may be collected:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Country
  • Country calling code
  • Service you are interested in
  • Message or enquiry
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your message

Limited technical information may also be received automatically when you visit the website, such as browser type, device type and website interaction data, depending on the hosting, analytics, security or other services connected to the website.

Sensitive personal information is not intentionally requested through the contact form. Please do not submit confidential legal, financial, medical, identification or other sensitive information through the website unless it is specifically requested and an appropriate secure method of communication has been provided.

2. Why We Collect Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand which service you are interested in
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry
  • Discuss potential work or services
  • Prepare an appropriate response or quotation
  • Provide requested information
  • Maintain records of enquiries
  • Improve the website and services
  • Protect the website against misuse or abuse

Your information will not be collected for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or notice.

3. Service Selection and Suggested Messages

The website may provide a suggested message based on the service you select. For example, selecting Tender & Procurement Support may automatically place a suggested enquiry into the message field. This is only a convenience feature, and you can edit, replace or remove the suggested message before submitting the form.

4. How We Protect Your Information

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, unauthorised disclosure, alteration and destruction. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Who May Receive Your Information

Information submitted through the website may be accessible to:

  • The website owner
  • Authorised personnel assisting with enquiries
  • Website hosting providers
  • Email or form processing providers
  • Other technical service providers required to operate the website

Where third party services process personal information on behalf of the website owner, appropriate measures should be taken to protect that information.

6. International Data Transfers

Some website, hosting, email, analytics or form processing providers may operate servers outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred or processed outside Kenya, appropriate safeguards should be considered and applied in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing prospective client relationships, maintaining appropriate business records and complying with legal obligations. When information is no longer required, reasonable steps are taken to securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is used
  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Exercise other rights provided under applicable Kenyan data protection law

Requests concerning your personal information may be made using the contact details below.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for functionality, security, analytics or user preferences. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced in the future, the website will provide appropriate notice and, where required, obtain the necessary consent.

10. Third Party Links

The website may contain links to third party websites, including professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn. There is no responsibility for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites, and you should review the privacy policies of third party websites before providing them with personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general professional and business use and is not specifically directed at children. Personal information is not knowingly requested from children through the contact form.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in services, website functionality, data processing practices, technology or legal and regulatory requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
